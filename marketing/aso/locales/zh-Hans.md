## Keyword rationale
- Name/Subtitle words are not repeated: 足球经理 (Name, covers 足球/经理) and 新赛季·开幕·战术·转会·青训·生涯·执教·俱乐部·夺冠 (Subtitle) are already indexed as one union, so keywords hold only distinct terms.
- 足球经理 in the Name is the exact category label CN players type for the football-management genre — the standard, unbranded genre keyword (the trademarked "Football Manager" is deliberately avoided; generic 足球经理 is what unbranded competitors use), so owning it in the Name anchors category search. Unchanged by the season pivot.
- **Subtitle re-pointed at the season window (was the tournament line), and it now runs at 25/30 instead of 21/30.** Chinese is character-dense, so the extra room buys whole head terms rather than syllables. The competitor read still holds — the deep-management incumbents stay evergreen/club and never lead on a *season* — so the line opens on 新赛季开幕 (season kickoff, the August intent), stacks four terms CN players actually type (战术 tactics, 转会 transfers, 青训 youth academy, 生涯 career), and closes on the benefit 执教俱乐部夺冠 (coach your club to the title) for the Today-tab ad format, which renders only icon + name + subtitle.
- Characters reclaimed from the tournament: the tournament token, the year and 国家队 left the Subtitle, and 转会·青训·生涯·执教·战术·俱乐部 left the keyword field (now covered by the Subtitle). That bought 主帅 (the CN word for a head coach alongside 教练), 国家队 (back into keywords now that the Subtitle no longer carries it), 保级 (the relegation-battle term, pairing with 升降级), 换人 (substitutions), 妖人 (CN football slang for a wonderkid — a real search word no rival owns), 免费 and 首发 (starting XI).
- 点球大战 was **dropped from the keyword field and moved into caption 2.** Captions are OCR-indexed since June 2025, so the standard Mandarin shootout term earns more as visible screenshot text than as five characters of a 100-character field — and the field now spends those characters on season and management intent instead.
- 无体力限制 has left the Subtitle. The no-timer wedge is a *conversion* argument, not a query: nobody searches it, so it now leads the Promotional Text and gets its own paragraph in the description opener, where it changes the install decision against the energy-gated gacha rivals.
- Management vocabulary mirrors the in-game tabs and CN football-sim search habits: 阵容/阵型 (squad/formation), 球探 (scout), 养成 (development), 升降级/保级, 教练/主帅, 引援 (signings — retained now that 转会 has moved to the Subtitle), 球队 (team, distinct from the Subtitle's 俱乐部).
- 模拟经营 and 战略 target the management/strategy-sim browse cluster; 卡包 ties pack-opening to the card-collecting search cluster; 冠军 and 名门 add trophy/prestige intent; 洲际赛 rides continental-cup intent without naming a real competition.
- Loop terms: 租借 (loan-deal system), 签约 (career negotiation), 比赛 (match — core sim word), 训练, 经营游戏 (management-game genre-browse phrase; 经营 is NOT in the Name, which carries 经理).
- 经理人 is retained — the exact zh-Hans head term Top Eleven owns in its own title ("Top Eleven 2026：成为全世界最优秀的足球经理人"), the zh-Hans-preferred variant (vs zh-Hant's 領隊). It is a distinct lexical unit (the 人-suffixed manager noun) and a generic descriptor, so it captures the 经理人-searcher segment the bare Name misses.
- Cross-store note: because zh-Hant serves TW/HK separately, this field targets Simplified-Chinese search only; the Traditional forms (球會/十二碼) live in zh-Hant so neither field wastes characters on the other's script.
- No tournament mark appears anywhere in consumer-visible copy (Guideline 2.3.7): the national-team lane is carried by the generic 国家队 / 国际大赛 / 洲际赛.
