## Keyword rationale
- In Australia "soccer" is the search term, not "football" — so it stays in the App Name; but a chunk of local fans do type "football", so it leads the Keywords field to capture both without wasting a repeat of the App Name word.
- Core management head terms (tactics, career, transfers, formation, squad, league) drive the bulk of sim search volume and anchor the field.
- "qualifiers" is an Australia-relevant World Cup 2026 hook — the Socceroos' qualifying run is a live news term in-market — and it does not appear in en-US, widening total English coverage.
- "national team" + "penalty shootout" ride the tournament window without repeating "World Cup" (already indexed via Subtitle), keeping every character earning.
- "manager" is intentionally excluded from Keywords because it already appears in the App Name (Apple indexes it); "coach" carries that intent in the Subtitle instead, which reads more naturally to Australian fans.
