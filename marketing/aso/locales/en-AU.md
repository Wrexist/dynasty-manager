- **App Name flipped "Soccer" → "Football".** The current-file position (AU searches "soccer") is defensible, but the fresh scan wins the tie: the category leader ships "Be Football Manager" on its GB/AU store title while switching to "Soccer" only for the US, and the research classes AU with GB as football-first. We hedge the risk by moving "soccer" to the leading Keyword slot, so soccer-search coverage is retained without spending an App Name word on it — exactly the research's "lead noun localised, secondary noun present for coverage" pattern. Worth A/B-watching install rank after the flip.
- **Subtitle now carries the anti-grind wedge** ("No Grind") in place of the "Be the Coach" role tag — the conversion line is the highest-leverage place for our most defensible differentiator, which the category leader structurally can't claim. "World Cup 2026" stays to hold the seasonal token every ranking rival stamps into its title/subtitle.
- Core management head terms (tactics, career, transfers, formation, squad, league) drive the bulk of sim search volume and anchor the field.
- "qualifiers" is an Australia-relevant World Cup 2026 hook — the Socceroos' qualifying run is a live in-market term — and it does not appear in en-US, widening total English coverage.
- **Added "academy" and "relegation".** Youth academy and the promotion/relegation pyramid are management-depth terms the arcade and card-collector rivals don't own — the durable counter to their real-player licence. They replace "penalty shootout" (a marquee screenshot feature but narrow search term, better sold visually) so every character keeps earning.
- "national team" rides the tournament window without repeating "World Cup" (already indexed via Subtitle); "coach" intent is carried by the Description rather than a Keyword.
