## Keyword rationale
- Name/Subtitle words are not repeated in keywords: サッカー・監督 (Name) and シーズン・開幕・采配・戦術・移籍・育成・クラブ・頂点 (Subtitle) are already indexed as one union, so the field spends every character on distinct high-intent terms.
- サッカー監督 in the Name is the exact genre head phrase JP fans search for football-management games — pairing サッカー (the dominant search word over フットボール, and the noun every serious competitor localizes into its title) with 監督 (kantoku — the authentic JP football word Sakatsuku uses in body copy and that Top Eleven's katakana-マネージャー title under-owns) captures sport and role in one indexed unit. Competitor evidence: Top Eleven's JP title is "サッカー マネージャー ゲーム" (loanword), so 監督 is the higher-authority, less-contested head term. This is unchanged by the season pivot.
- **Subtitle re-pointed at the season window (was the tournament line).** The same competitor read still holds — the deep-management incumbents (Top Eleven evergreen, Sakatsuku J-League-only) do not sell a *season*, they sell an endless club. So the Subtitle now opens on 新シーズン開幕 (season kickoff, the August intent) and then spends the remaining characters on four head terms JP players actually type: 采配 (touchline command), 戦術 (tactics), 移籍 (transfers), 育成 (youth development — one of the highest-volume JP sim search words). It still reads as a benefit sentence for the Today-tab ad format, which renders only icon + name + subtitle.
- Characters reclaimed from the tournament: the tournament token, the year and 代表 left the Subtitle, and 采配・移籍・育成・クラブ left the keyword field (now covered by the Subtitle). That paid for 代表 (national team — generic, no mark), 補強 (squad reinforcement, the JP transfer-window verb), スタジアム (stadium/facilities) and 無料 (the 無料サッカーゲーム browse query, which the free-to-download build can honestly answer), plus room to keep 降格 and 契約.
- pk戦 was **dropped from the keyword field and moved into caption 2.** Captions are OCR-indexed since June 2025, so a short, visually obvious feature term belongs there rather than in the 100-character field — which now buys season and management terms instead.
- Genre-defining nouns mirror the in-game tabs and JP football-sim vocabulary: フォーメーション, スカウト, ユース, 昇格/降格 (the full promotion-relegation cluster), 契約 (career-mode negotiation), 試合 (match — the core sim search word), 経営, キャリア, リーグ.
- 名門 (prestigious club), シミュレーション and 戦略 round out genre intent for players browsing management/strategy sims; カード ties the pack-opening feature to the collectible-card search cluster.
- マネージャー is retained — the exact katakana head term Top Eleven owns in its own JP title ("サッカー マネージャー ゲーム"); it is generic/genre (not a brand string) and NOT a substring of the Name's 監督, so it captures the loanword-searcher segment the 監督 Name misses.
- Excluded サッカーゲーム/監督ゲーム despite volume — both duplicate a Name substring (サッカー/監督) and would waste characters Apple already indexes.
- No tournament mark appears anywhere in consumer-visible copy (Guideline 2.3.7): the national-team lane is carried by the generic 代表 / 国際大会 / 大陸カップ.
