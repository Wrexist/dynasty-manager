## Keyword rationale
- Name/Subtitle words are not repeated in keywords: サッカー・監督 (Name) and ワールドカップ・2026・代表・戦術・王朝 (Subtitle) are already indexed, so the field spends every character on distinct high-intent terms.
- サッカー監督 in the Name is the exact genre head phrase JP fans search for football-management games — pairing サッカー (the dominant search word over フットボール, and the noun every serious competitor localizes into its title) with 監督 (kantoku — the authentic JP football word Sakatsuku uses in body copy and that Top Eleven's katakana-マネージャー title under-owns) captures sport and role in one indexed unit. Competitor evidence: Top Eleven's JP title is "サッカー マネージャー ゲーム" (loanword), so 監督 is the higher-authority, less-contested head term.
- pk戦 (the standard JP shorthand for a penalty shootout; katakana PK is how fans actually type it) carries the World Cup window; 国際大会 rides international-tournament intent without naming a real competition.
- **Subtitle rewritten as the conversion line (competitor-driven):** the deep-management incumbents (Top Eleven evergreen, Sakatsuku J-League-only) do NOT foreground the World Cup or a national-team job, so "代表を率い" (lead the national team) opens the WC-2026 national-team lane the research flags as an open management-app wedge, while 王朝 (dynasty, brand noun) is retained. 代表 moved out of the keyword field into the Subtitle so it stays indexed without spending a keyword character; 戦術 (tactics) likewise indexes via the Subtitle.
- Genre-defining verbs mirror the in-game tabs and JP football-sim vocabulary: 采配 (touchline command), 育成 (youth development — a huge sim search term), 移籍 (transfers), フォーメーション, スカウト, 昇格 (promotion).
- 名門 (prestigious club), シミュレーション and 戦略 round out genre intent for players browsing management/strategy sims; カード ties the pack-opening feature to the collectible-card search cluster.
- Management-loop terms fill the field: 優勝 (winning the title), 降格 (relegation — pairs with 昇格 for the full promotion/relegation cluster), 契約 (contract — career-mode negotiation), 試合 (match — the core sim search word).
- **New this pass (competitor-driven):** added マネージャー — the exact katakana head term Top Eleven owns in its own JP title ("サッカー マネージャー ゲーム"); it is generic/genre (not a brand string) and NOT a substring of the Name's 監督, so it captures the loanword-searcher segment the 監督 Name misses. Dropped the weaker 戦力 (overlaps 選手) and removed 代表 (now carried by the Subtitle) to make room while staying at 100/100.
- Excluded サッカーゲーム/監督ゲーム despite volume — both duplicate a Name substring (サッカー/監督) and would waste characters Apple already indexes.
