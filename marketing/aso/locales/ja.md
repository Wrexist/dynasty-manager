## Keyword rationale
- Name/Subtitle words are not repeated in keywords: サッカー・監督 (Name) and ワールドカップ・2026・戦術・制覇 (Subtitle) are already indexed, so the field spends every character on distinct high-intent terms.
- サッカー監督 in the Name is the exact genre head phrase JP fans search for football-management games — pairing サッカー (the dominant search word over フットボール) with 監督 (manager/gaffer) captures both the sport and the role in one indexed unit.
- 代表 (national team) and pk戦 (the standard JP shorthand for a penalty shootout; katakana PK is how fans actually type it) carry the World Cup window; 国際大会 rides international-tournament intent without naming a real competition.
- Genre-defining verbs mirror the in-game tabs and JP football-sim vocabulary: 采配 (touchline command), 育成 (youth development — a huge sim search term), 移籍 (transfers), フォーメーション, スカウト, 昇格 (promotion).
- 名門 (prestigious club), シミュレーション and 戦略 round out genre intent for players browsing management/strategy sims; カード ties the pack-opening feature to the collectible-card search cluster.
- Enrichment pass added four distinct management-loop terms that fill the field to 99/100: 優勝 (winning the title — distinct from Subtitle's 制覇), 降格 (relegation — pairs with the existing 昇格 for the full promotion/relegation cluster), 契約 (contract — career-mode negotiation intent) and 試合 (match — the core sim search word). None duplicate a Name/Subtitle substring.
- Subtitle strengthened to add 王朝 (dynasty) — the brand's core noun, previously indexed nowhere — without hurting readability ("戦術で制覇し王朝を築け" reads as a natural imperative line); it is deliberately kept out of the keyword field so no character is spent twice.
- Excluded サッカーゲーム/監督ゲーム despite volume — both duplicate a Name substring (サッカー/監督) and would waste characters Apple already indexes.
