## Keyword rationale
- **Season pivot (2026-08):** the subtitle no longer carries "WK 2026". The tournament closed on 19 July 2026 and its token was sitting in the second-most-weighted field we own — the one Apple's Today-tab ads render. It is replaced with "jouw carrière", so the subtitle now claims the two terms this market actually types year-round.
- The head compound "voetbalmanager" stays in the **Subtitle**, unchanged from the previous refresh: it is the undisputed genre term in this market, OSM owns it in NL, and it belongs in the highest-weighted field to contest head-on. "carrière" joins it — career-mode intent is a real query cluster and is what the app is all year, not just in a tournament summer.
- Because "voetbalmanager", "Voetbal", "Manager" and "carrière" now live in the App Name/Subtitle, they stay out of Keywords (Apple indexes the union of the three fields, so a repeat is a wasted character). "carriere" was therefore dropped from the field.
- Characters reclaimed from the retired tournament terms went to "seizoen" (the window itself), "beker" (domestic-cup intent, previously cut for room) and "jeugd" (jeugdopleiding / talent development — a differentiator against the arcade clones). "nationaal" was retired with the tournament: the national-team job is still in the build and still sells, but it now earns its indexing from screenshot caption 05 rather than from the 100-char field.
- "spel" is kept to reclaim OSM's other high-value string: it recombines with the name's "Voetbal"/"Manager" to cover "voetbal spel" and "voetbal manager spel" — the space-separated phrasing OSM literally puts in its own App Store title ("nr. 1 voetbal spel").
- "coach" matches OSM's own positioning verb ("word de coach"); "elftal", "opstelling", "strafschoppen", "selectie" are the exact Dutch football words for the eleven, lineup, penalties and squad; "competitie" and "transfers" are high-volume category heads.
- The wedge — no energy timers, no rest packs, full depth without a subscription — is deliberately NOT in the keyword field. Nobody searches "geen energiebalk". It lives where it changes the install decision: the promotional text opener and the description's third paragraph, both of which land above the "meer" fold.
- Screenshot captions are an indexed field (Apple OCR-extracts them since June 2025), so they carry terms the 100-char field cannot afford: "wedstrijddag", "formatie", "chemie", "transfermarkt", "scout", "bondscoach".
- Description keeps the local-relevance hook but phrases it generically – no real league or club appears in any store field, and the national-team line says "een wereldtoernooi", never a tournament mark.
