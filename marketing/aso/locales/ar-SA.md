## Keyword rationale
- "كورة" (colloquial for football, how most Arab fans casually type it) is a high-volume head term deliberately included — it captures the massive informal-search segment that "كرة القدم" (formal, already in the App Name) misses.
- Name/Subtitle words are not repeated: "كرة القدم", "مدرب", "كأس", "العالم" and "2026" are already indexed, so keywords focus on distinct high-intent terms.
- "منتخب" (national team) and "ركلات ترجيح" (penalty shootout) carry the World Cup window; "ركلات ترجيح" is the correct Gulf/MENA idiom for a shootout, far more searched than a literal rendering.
- Core management verbs Arab fans search — "تكتيك" (tactics), "تشكيلة" (lineup), "انتقالات" (transfers), "دوري" (league) — mirror the in-game tabs and the language of Arabic football media.
- "دوري الأبطال" (Champions-style continental cup, generic) rides a very high-volume phrase without naming any real competition; "مسيرة" (career) and "محاكاة" (simulation) round out genre intent.
- Because ar-SA is Apple's only Arabic localization, this single field must serve Gulf, Levantine and North-African search habits at once — hence the mix of formal (كرة القدم in Name) and colloquial (كورة in keywords).
