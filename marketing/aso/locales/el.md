## Keyword rationale
- **Window change: the tournament framing is gone.** "Μουντιάλ" — the loanword Greeks actually search, which beat the formal "Παγκόσμιο Κύπελλο" — was the right lead until 19 July 2026 and is dead weight now. The Subtitle spends all 27 characters on the season window instead.
- **"Μάνατζερ" stays in the Subtitle**, which is the whole reason the old subtitle worked: it is the transliterated head term Greeks type for a manager game, Top Eleven underweights it, and with App Name "Ποδόσφαιρο" it reconstructs "ποδοσφαιρικός μάνατζερ" — a phrase no localized rival owns. "καριέρα & τακτική" fills the freed characters with the two highest-volume category head terms and reads as a benefit in the Today-tab ad format (name + icon + subtitle only).
- **Keywords reclaimed from the tournament:** "παγκοσμιο" existed only to reconstruct the formal tournament name with "κυπελλο", so it is gone. "καριερα" and "τακτικη" also left the field — they moved up into the Subtitle, and Apple indexes App Name + Subtitle + Keywords as one set, so a repeat is a wasted character (the unaccented keyword form normalises to the accented subtitle word, so this would have failed validation).
- Those three freed ~28 characters, now funding "σεζον", "ακαδημια" and "σχηματισμος" — season, youth academy and formation, which are the club-season queries this window gets.
- "εθνικη", "πεναλτι", "ενδεκαδα", "μεταγραφη", "κυπελλο", "συλλογος", "προπονητης" are the natural Greek words for national team, penalty, starting XI, transfer, cup, club and coach — culturally correct over any calque. Now singular ("μεταγραφη", not "μεταγραφες") since Apple matches the plural and the singular is a character cheaper.
- All Keywords are unaccented (no tonos: "εθνικη" not "εθνική") because Greek users routinely omit accents in search; Apple matches the accented form too, so it is free coverage.
- "Μάνατζερ", "ποδόσφαιρο", "καριέρα" and "τακτική" are omitted from Keywords — they sit in the App Name/Subtitle and are indexed separately. No plurals of included singulars, no club/league proper nouns, no category name.
- The captions carry what 100 characters cannot: "μεταγραφική αγορά", "σκάουτινγκ", "συμβόλαια", "χημεία", "λεπτό προς λεπτό" and "ηπειρωτικές διοργανώσεις" — all indexed since Apple began OCR-extracting screenshot text.
