## Keyword rationale
- "менеджер" is the head term Ukrainians type for a management game; "мундіаль" captures the second everyday name for the World Cup (alongside "ЧС" in the Subtitle), doubling tournament coverage.
- "збірна", "пенальті", "склад", "трансфери", "кубок", "чемпіонат" are the natural Ukrainian words for national team, penalty, lineup, transfers, cup and championship — culturally correct, not calques from English or Russian.
- "кар'єра" keeps the apostrophe because that is the correct Ukrainian spelling users type on the native keyboard; Cyrillic script has no accent-stripping concern.
- "Футбол", "футбольний", "тренер" are omitted from Keywords — they sit in the App Name/Subtitle and Apple indexes those separately.
- "тактика", "ліга", "клуб" anchor the field as high-volume management-sim head terms. No plurals of included singulars, no club/league proper nouns.
