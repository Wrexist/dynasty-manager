- Трансфери й контракти: шукай таланти через скаутів, домовляйся про суми та зарплати, оформлюй оренди, тримай зарплатню під контролем

## Keyword rationale
- **Window change: the tournament framing is gone.** The 2026 tournament ended 19 July; for the 2026-27 club season the intent is "run my club for a season", so the Subtitle spends all 28 characters on evergreen head terms instead of a dead token.
- **The Subtitle still binds "футбольний менеджер"** — the exact native head phrase the ua storefront leaves uncontested because every big rival ships Russian metadata there. An in-subtitle placement outranks a keyword-field token, so this keeps the empty Ukrainian lane claimed. "кар'єра" joins it: career-mode intent is the season-window equivalent of tournament intent, and the pair reads as a benefit in the Today-tab ad format (name + icon + subtitle only).
- "кар'єра" keeps the apostrophe — the correct Ukrainian spelling users type on the native keyboard; Cyrillic has no accent-stripping concern.
- **Keywords reclaimed from the tournament:** dropping "мундіаль" (the everyday second name for the tournament) and "пенальті" freed the characters now funding "сезон", "симулятор", "схема" and "скаут" — season, sim, formation and scouting are the queries this window actually gets.
- "кар'єра" moved out of Keywords the moment it entered the Subtitle — Apple indexes App Name + Subtitle + Keywords as one set, so a repeat is a wasted character.
- **"онлайн" retained** — OSM's ua listing is "Футбольний онлайн менеджер", so "онлайн" harvests that high-intent pattern around the in-subtitle phrase. "тренер" stays in Keywords.
- "збірна", "склад", "трансфер", "кубок", "чемпіонат" are the natural Ukrainian words for national team, lineup, transfer, cup and championship — not calques from English or Russian. Singulars only ("трансфер", not "трансфери") — Apple matches the plural.
- "Футбол", "футбольний", "менеджер", "кар'єра" are omitted from Keywords — they sit in the App Name/Subtitle and are indexed separately. No plurals of included singulars, no club/league proper nouns, no category name.
- The captions carry what the 100-char field cannot afford: "трансферне вікно", "хімія команди", "скаути", "контракти", "континентальні турніри" and "пенальті" — all indexed since Apple began OCR-extracting screenshot text.
