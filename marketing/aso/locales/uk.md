## Keyword rationale
- **Biggest change: "футбольний менеджер" moved into the Subtitle** (replacing "футбольний тренер"). It's the exact native head phrase the ua storefront leaves uncontested because every big rival ships Russian metadata there; an in-title placement outranks a keyword-field token, so this claims the empty Ukrainian lane.
- **New: "онлайн" added** — OSM's ua listing is "Футбольний онлайн менеджер", so "онлайн" harvests that high-intent pattern around the in-title phrase. "тренер" (coach) drops into Keywords now that the old subtitle no longer carries it.
- "мундіаль" captures the second everyday name for the World Cup (alongside "ЧС" in the Subtitle), doubling tournament coverage; "збірна", "пенальті", "склад", "трансфери", "кубок", "чемпіонат" are the natural Ukrainian words for national team, penalty, lineup, transfers, cup and championship — not calques from English or Russian.
- "кар'єра" keeps the apostrophe — the correct Ukrainian spelling users type on the native keyboard; Cyrillic has no accent-stripping concern.
- "Футбол", "футбольний", "менеджер" are omitted from Keywords — they sit in the App Name/Subtitle and are indexed separately. Dropped the weakest terms ("гол", "пас") to fund "тренер"/"онлайн". No plurals of included singulars, no club/league proper nouns.
