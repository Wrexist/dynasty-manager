## Keyword rationale
- **Subtitle pivot.** "VB 2026" is gone — the tournament ended 19 July 2026, and on the Today-tab ad format (name + icon + subtitle only) that token was doing all the selling. The Subtitle keeps **"futball menedzser" as two words**, which remains the right call for the same reason as before: it is OSM's exact HU title phrase and the term Top Eleven leaves in English here, and two tokens cover more query shapes ("futball menedzser", "menedzser", and with the App Name's "foci" also "foci menedzser"). The reclaimed characters go to **karrier** — where intent moves once the club season starts.
- **Reclaimed characters.** "karrier" left the keyword field because it is now in the Subtitle (Apple indexes Name + Subtitle + Keywords as one union, so a repeat is a wasted character and a validator failure), and "valogatott" and "tizenegyes" left with the tournament window. That freed ~30 characters, now spent on **szimulator, szezon, utanpotlas, bajnok** — the season-window cluster: sim depth, the season itself, the youth academy, and the title race.
- **The compound "focimenedzser" stays in Keywords** as a distinct token from the two-word subtitle phrase, so both registers rank. "labdarugas" holds the formal register while the everyday "foci" sits in the App Name — both covered, no duplication.
- "atigazolas", "keret", "kupa", "edzo" are the exact Hungarian words for transfer, squad, cup and coach — culturally correct, not literal translations of English head terms.
- Unaccented forms throughout ("labdarugas", "atigazolas", "szimulator", "utanpotlas") match how Hungarians type without ékezet; visible copy keeps full accents.
- "foci", "futball", "menedzser", "karrier" are absent from Keywords — already indexed via App Name/Subtitle.
- The terms that lost their keyword slot moved to the screenshot captions, which Apple OCR-indexes as metadata (since June 2025): **válogatott**, **tizenegyespárbaj**, **karriermód**, **meccsnap**, **átigazolási időszak**, **kontinentális kupák**. These are exactly the full-length compounds the 100-character field cannot afford.
- No plurals of included singulars, no club/league proper nouns, no tournament marks in consumer-visible copy (Guideline 2.3.7) — the description says "világtorna", never the mark.
