## Keyword rationale
- "fussball" (unaccented ss form) is included per the mandate: countless DACH users type ss instead of ß, and Apple treats it as a distinct token from the App Name's "Fußball". "fussballmanager" is THE head compound in this market — German fans search the solid compound noun, not two words — so it earns its 15 characters.
- Terms already in the App Name/Subtitle (Fußball, Manager, WM, 2026, Trainer, Taktik) are omitted — Apple indexes those separately, so repeating them wastes characters.
- "elfmeter", "aufstellung", "kader", "verein", "pokal" and "nationalmannschaft" are the exact words German fans use for penalties, lineup, squad, club, cup and national team — culturally correct over any literal translation, and they ride the WM window without repeating "WM".
- "liga" and "karriere" are high-volume category head terms shared across every management sim; they anchor the field. No plurals of included singulars, no club/league proper nouns.
- Safe trademark alternate (near-zero IP exposure): swap "WM 2026" for "Weltmeisterschaft 2026" — already a generic championship term in German (not a FIFA mark), so DACH metadata carries little exposure either way.
