## Keyword rationale
- Canada uses "soccer" for search, so it anchors the App Name; "football" leads the Keywords field to catch the sizeable bilingual/immigrant fan base that types it, without repeating an App Name word.
- North American vocabulary is chosen deliberately to differ from en-US's list where the storefront may cross-index en-US as a fallback: "roster" (vs squad), "signings" (vs transfers), "penalty kicks" (vs penalty shootout), "knockout" and "dugout" are all distinct terms, so the union of en-CA + en-US metadata covers more surface than either alone.
- Core sim head terms (tactics, career, formation, league) are retained because they are the demand centre of the category and can't be sacrificed for pure differentiation.
- 2026 hosting is a Canada/North America storyline, so the Subtitle leans on "Lead Your Club" and the Description opens on the tournament arriving on the continent — locally resonant without naming any real competition body.
- "manager" is excluded from Keywords (already in App Name and indexed); the career-mode intent is carried by "career" and the Description copy instead.
