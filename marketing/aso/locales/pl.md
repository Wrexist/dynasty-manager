## Keyword rationale
- **Biggest change: the Subtitle is now `Taktyka, Transfery i Kariera`** — three evergreen category head terms that read as a benefit in the Today-tab ad format (name + icon + subtitle only). The seasonal tournament token that used to occupy this line is gone: that window closed on 19 July 2026, and tournament names — colloquial and formal Polish alike — are registered marks we keep out of every consumer-visible field (APP_STORE_LISTING.md risk #4).
- **Open question for the next pl pass:** "menedżer piłkarski" is the exact descriptor the category incumbent localizes into its own Polish title, and a Subtitle placement outranks a keyword-field token. It currently sits in NEITHER field. Contesting it head-on would mean spending most of the 30-character Subtitle on it — worth testing against the three head terms now there.
- Because the manager head words are covered by the App Name lock-up and the Subtitle, Keywords drop them and re-spend the freed budget on complementary terms; no tournament token is bought back.
- "reprezentacja" now carries national-team intent on its own — the generic word ranks year-round and costs nothing in IP exposure; "trener" moved into Keywords now that the old "trener i taktyka" subtitle is gone.
- "karne", "reprezentacja", "sklad", "transfery", "kariera" are the exact Polish words for penalties, national team, lineup, transfers and career — culturally correct, not literal translations of the English.
- Unaccented forms throughout ("sklad" not "skład") match how Poles type queries without diacritics; the visible copy keeps full Polish spelling.
- "futbol" is kept alongside the App Name's "piłka nożna" because a meaningful slice of younger fans search the borrowed word; Apple treats it as a distinct token.
- Dropped the weakest terms ("gol", "formacja") to make room. Excluded: plurals of included singulars, any club/league proper nouns, and words already in the App Name/Subtitle.
