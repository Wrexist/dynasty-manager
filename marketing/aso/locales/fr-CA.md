## Keyword rationale
- **Season refresh (2026-07):** the field now carries `simulation` and drops the tournament terms the closed window was funding. Rationale:
  - The no-subscription wedge is a CONVERSION message, not a search one — nobody searches "sans abonnement". It lives in the Promotional Text and the Description close, where it changes the install decision, and the keyword characters go to head terms instead. (An earlier draft of this file claimed the phrase was in the keyword field; it is not, and should not be.)
  - Dropped **`transferts`** because "transactions" (the Québec transfer word, in the Description) plus the en-CA English field already cover buy/sell intent; the character budget is better spent on the subscription wedge.
  - `simulation` IS carried here despite being near-identical in English. Canada indexes en-CA + fr-CA as a union, so the duplication is a real (small) waste — flagged for the next fr-CA pass, along with the `club`/`formation` overlap with en-CA. Everything else in the field is French-specific and unreachable from the English one.
- Québec says "soccer", so the brand descriptor uses it and anchors the App Name. That frees "football" as a keyword — a chunk of Canadian search still types it, and it doesn't collide with the name.
- Québec football vocabulary differs from France and is what wins this market: "tirs de barrage" (penalty shootout — France's "tirs au but" would feel foreign here), "alignement" (starting lineup, vs France's "compo"), plus Québec-flavoured copy in the Description ("entrevues", "congédiement", "à la demie", "coach").
- Because the Canadian storefront also indexes the en-CA field, this field should carry French-specific queries (accentless "carriere", "alignement", "selection", "jeune") the English field can't reach. `club`, `formation` and `simulation` currently duplicate en-CA and are the first candidates to swap out.
- The seasonal tournament terms that used to lead this field and sit in the Subtitle are gone: the 2026 window closed on 19 July, so the Subtitle now carries the manager/squad/transfer head terms and the Promo carries the 2026-27 season hook. "selection" holds national-team intent generically, at a fraction of the characters and none of the IP exposure.
- Excluded competitor, league, club and player names (IP). Excluded "soccer" and "entraîneur" from keywords since they're already in the Name/Subtitle.
- No tournament name goes in any consumer-visible field, in any language — they are registered marks (APP_STORE_LISTING.md risk #4). The in-game mode is described generically in the visible copy ("tournois internationaux", "sélections nationales").
