## Keyword rationale
- "מונדיאל" is the word Israeli fans actually use for the World Cup (borrowed, universal in Hebrew football culture) — it anchors the Subtitle, so the keyword field avoids repeating it and spends characters elsewhere.
- "פנדלים" is the colloquial Hebrew for penalties/shootout that fans type, chosen over the formal "בעיטות עונשין" used in body prose; it rides the shootout hook without duplicating any Name/Subtitle word.
- Core genre + verb terms: "נבחרת" (national team), "טקטיקה" (tactics), "הרכב" (lineup), "העברות" (transfers), "ליגה" (league), "קריירה" (career), "ניהול" (management) — the exact words an Israeli football fan searches, mirroring in-game tabs.
- "כדורגל" is in the App Name (indexed), so it is omitted from keywords; "מאמן" (coach) is carried in the description rather than keywords to leave room, since "ניהול"/"נבחרת" cover the higher-intent management searches.
- "אצטדיון" (stadium), "טורניר" (tournament) and "גביע" (cup) fill the field to 97/100 with adjacent-intent terms that catch broader football-app browsing.
