## Keyword rationale
- Subtitle change: switched from the verb-led "נהל את המונדיאל 2026" to the noun phrase "מאמן נבחרת המונדיאל 2026". This claims "מאמן" — the native, largely UNCONTESTED Hebrew head term for a football coach (Top Eleven only contests the transliterated "מנג'ר") — plus "נבחרת" (national team) in the highest-weighted field, while still anchoring on "מונדיאל" and the WC window. Reads as an identity hook: you are the World Cup 2026 national-team coach.
- NEW: added "מנג'ר" (transliterated "manager", gershayim included) — the exact head term Top Eleven ranks on and the one Israeli fans type. Since the Subtitle now owns the native "מאמן", the keyword field flanks the transliterated variant so we cover both spellings of the head term.
- Removed "נבחרת" from keywords (now carried in the Subtitle, so keeping it would duplicate); those characters went to "מנג'ר".
- "פנדלים" is the colloquial Hebrew for a shootout that fans type (over the formal "בעיטות עונשין"), riding the shootout hook without duplicating any Name/Subtitle word.
- Core genre + verb terms — "טקטיקה", "הרכב", "העברות", "ליגה", "קריירה", "ניהול" (the "ניהול קבוצת כדורגל" intent, paired with "קבוצה") — mirror the in-game tabs and the words an Israeli fan searches.
- "כדורגל" and "מאמן" are in the Name/Subtitle (indexed), so both are omitted from keywords; "אצטדיון", "טורניר" and "גביע" fill the field with adjacent-intent browsing terms.
- Description change: the anti-grind + no-subscription wedge moves to line 2 so it lands in the pre-"more" preview — the one thing incumbents structurally can't say (Top Eleven hides its energy/rest-pack grind; the premier deep sim requires a paid subscription to play). Promo/description say "בלי צורך במנוי" (no need for a subscription) rather than "no subscription", staying accurate to the optional Dynasty Pro plan.
