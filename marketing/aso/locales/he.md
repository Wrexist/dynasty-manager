Storefronts reached: Israel (he storefront). Single-storefront localization; Hebrew is the device language for the Israeli football-gaming audience. RTL-safe: brand stays Latin left-to-right, all descriptor/body copy is native Hebrew and no Latin words are mixed into RTL runs where they would flip the punctuation. This is our lowest-competition ASO opening: only Top Eleven localizes to Hebrew (on the transliterated "מנג'ר"); most other manager sims stay English in the IL store, so the native head terms "מאמן כדורגל" and "ניהול קבוצת כדורגל" are cheap to own with a properly localized Name + Subtitle. Window: the 2026-27 club season — European leagues restarted in August and the summer transfer window shuts on 1 September.

## Keyword rationale
- **Season pivot (2026-08):** the Subtitle and Promotional Text no longer carry the tournament token. The tournament ended 19 July 2026 and the token was burning the second-most-weighted field we own — the field Apple's Today-tab ads render. The Subtitle is now `מאמן: טקטיקה, העברות, קריירה`, which keeps the "מאמן" claim and spends the reclaimed characters on three category head terms that are true all year.
- **Why "מאמן" stays first:** it is the native, largely UNCONTESTED Hebrew head term for a football coach (Top Eleven only contests the transliterated "מנג'ר"), and it belongs in the highest-weighted field we can change without a build. Everything after the colon is now club-season intent rather than tournament intent.
- Because "כדורגל", "מאמן", "טקטיקה", "העברות" and "קריירה" now sit in the Name/Subtitle, they are excluded from Keywords — Apple indexes the union of the three fields, so a repeat is a wasted character. This is the usual way this file starts failing validation after a Subtitle change; re-check it every refresh.
- **"מנג'ר"** (transliterated "manager", gershayim included) stays in the field: it is the exact head term Top Eleven ranks on and the one many Israeli fans type. The Subtitle owns the native spelling, the field flanks the transliterated one, so both spellings of the head term are covered.
- Characters reclaimed from the retired tournament terms bought **"עונה"** (season — the window itself), **"מועדון"** (club, now that the copy is club-first), **"נוער"** (the youth academy, a real differentiator against the arcade titles) and **"אליפות"** (title race). **"נבחרת"** returns to the field: it left last refresh only because the Subtitle carried it, and the national-team job is still in the build year-round.
- "פנדלים" is the colloquial Hebrew for a shootout that fans type (over the formal "בעיטות עונשין"), and it duplicates nothing in the Name/Subtitle.
- Core genre and verb terms — "הרכב", "ליגה", "ניהול" (the "ניהול קבוצת כדורגל" intent, paired with "קבוצה"), "סימולציה", "שחקנים", "אימון" — mirror the in-game tabs and the words an Israeli fan searches. "אצטדיון" and "גביע" fill the field with adjacent-intent browsing terms.
- The wedge — no energy timers, no rest packs, full depth without a subscription — is deliberately kept OUT of the keyword field; nobody searches "בלי מדי אנרגיה". It lives in the promotional text and in the third paragraph of the description, both above the fold, where it changes the install decision. It is the one thing the incumbents structurally cannot say: Top Eleven hides an energy/rest-pack grind, and the premier deep sim requires a paid subscription to play. The copy says "בלי צורך במנוי" (no need for a subscription) rather than "no subscription", staying accurate to the optional Dynasty Pro plan and to the free-to-download claim.
- Screenshot captions are an indexed field (Apple OCR-extracts them since June 2025) and carry what the 100-char field cannot afford: "יום משחק", "סגל", "מערך", "כימיה", "סקאוטינג", "מוקדמות".
- No real club, league, player or tournament mark appears in any store field. The national-team line says "טורניר עולמי", a generic descriptor. The previous tournament copy is preserved in git history for the next window.
