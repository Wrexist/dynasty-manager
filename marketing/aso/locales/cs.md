## Keyword rationale
- **Subtitle pivot.** "MS 2026" is gone — the tournament ended 19 July 2026 and the token was burning the second-most-weighted field we own. The Subtitle keeps the exact CZ head phrase "fotbalový manažer" (the term LFM built its title around and one Top Eleven does localize on CZ) and spends the reclaimed characters on **kariéra**, which is where intent moves once the club season starts. In-title placement is worth roughly 5× the same word in Keywords, so the head phrase stays here and Keywords fund the tail.
- **Reclaimed characters.** "mistrovstvi" (the formal WC name) and "reprezentace" both left the keyword field; "kariera" left it too because it is now in the Subtitle and Apple indexes Name + Subtitle + Keywords as one union. That freed ~30 characters, which now buy **sezona, simulator, akademie, skaut, postup** — the season-window cluster (season, sim depth, youth academy, scouting, promotion).
- **"online" stays.** It is the single most valuable CZ modifier we hold: reconstructing "online fotbalový manažer" against the in-title phrase. Nothing about the season change weakens it.
- "penalta" replaces the plural "penalty" — Apple matches plurals from the singular, so the plural was paying for a character it did not need. Same reasoning turned "prestupy" into "prestup".
- Unaccented forms ("prestup", "pohar", "trener", "sezona", "akademie") match how Czechs type without háček/čárka; visible copy keeps full diacritics throughout.
- "fotbal", "fotbalový", "manažer", "kariéra" are absent from Keywords — they sit in the App Name/Subtitle and Apple indexes those separately, so a repeat would be a wasted character (and a validator failure).
- National-team intent did not disappear, it moved: **reprezentace** and **kontinentální poháry** now live in screenshot caption 5, which Apple OCR-indexes since June 2025. Same for "kariérní režim", "den zápasu", "přestupové okno" and "penaltový rozstřel" — terms the 100-character field cannot afford at full length.
- No plurals of included singulars, no club/league proper nouns, no tournament marks anywhere in consumer-visible copy (Guideline 2.3.7) — the description says "světový turnaj", never the mark.
