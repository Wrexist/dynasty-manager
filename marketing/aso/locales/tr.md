## Keyword rationale
- "teknik direktor" is the dominant Turkish search head term for football-management games (the DT role) — placed first, unaccented ("direktor" not "direktör") because Turkish users routinely type without diacritics on iOS.
- "menajer" is deliberately excluded: the Subtitle already carries "Menajeri" and the App Name carries "Futbol", so the phrase "futbol menajeri" is already indexed — repeating it would burn characters.
- "kadro / transfer / taktik / diziliş" are the core in-game verbs Turkish fans search (squad, transfer, tactics, lineup) and mirror the tab names local players expect.
- "milli takim" (national team) rides the World Cup window; "penalti" captures the shootout hook without duplicating any Name/Subtitle word.
- "antrenor" (coach) added as a synonym head term alongside "teknik direktor" to catch users who search the shorter word; "puan durumu" (league table) trimmed to stay under 100 while keeping the highest-intent terms.
