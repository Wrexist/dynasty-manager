## Keyword rationale
- UK/IE fans search "football", never "soccer", so the App Name descriptor is "Football" and "soccer" is deliberately excluded — it would waste characters on a term this market doesn't type.
- Head terms "tactics", "career", "transfers", "formation", "squad" and "league" are the highest-volume management-sim queries and carry across every football title; they anchor the field.
- British-specific terms "fixtures", "promotion" and "gaffer" (in the Subtitle) map to how UK fans describe the season and the manager role — coverage rivals miss.
- "national team" + "penalty shootout" ride the World Cup 2026 window without repeating "World Cup" (already indexed via Subtitle) — Apple indexes Name/Subtitle separately, so those words are omitted from Keywords to save space.
- No plurals of included singulars and no competitor/club/league proper nouns — pure category and culture terms only.
