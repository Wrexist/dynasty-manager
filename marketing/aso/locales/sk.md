Dynasty Manager je hĺbková futbalová manažérska simulácia – skutočná trénerská kariéra, nie zbieranie kariet. Nastav taktiku, odpracuj prestupové okno a rieš každé striedanie, kým zápas beží minútu po minúte.

Žiadne ukazovatele energie. Žiadne oddychové balíčky. Žiadne čakanie medzi hraním. Hraj, koľko chceš a kedy chceš.

## Keyword rationale
- **Subtitle pivot.** "MS 2026" is gone — the tournament ended 19 July 2026 and the token was sitting in the field Apple's Today-tab ad renders. The Subtitle keeps "futbalový manažér", the native head phrase Top Eleven ("Be a Soccer Manager") and DLS both leave in English on the SK storefront; that untranslated gap is the reason this phrase stays in-title rather than in the weaker keyword field. The reclaimed characters go to **kariéra**, which is where intent moves once the club season starts.
- **Reclaimed characters.** "majstrovstva" (the formal WC name) and "reprezentacia" left the keyword field, and "kariera" left it too because it is now in the Subtitle — Apple indexes Name + Subtitle + Keywords as one union, so a repeat is both a wasted character and a validator failure. That freed ~33 characters, now spent on **sezona, simulator, akademia, skaut, postup**: the season-window cluster (season, sim depth, youth academy, scouting, promotion).
- **"online" stays.** It harvests the "online futbalový manažér" pattern against the in-title phrase — a modifier the season change does not weaken.
- "penalta" replaces the plural "penalty"; Apple matches plurals from the singular, so the plural was paying for a character it did not need. Same reasoning turned "prestupy" into "prestup".
- Unaccented forms ("prestup", "trener", "pohar", "sezona", "akademia") match phone typing without mäkčeň/dĺžeň; visible copy keeps full Slovak diacritics.
- "futbal", "futbalový", "manažér", "kariéra" are absent from Keywords — they are already indexed via App Name/Subtitle.
- National-team intent moved rather than disappeared: **reprezentácia** and **kontinentálne poháre** now sit in screenshot caption 5, and Apple OCR-indexes caption text as metadata (since June 2025). "kariérny režim", "deň zápasu", "prestupové okno" and "penaltový rozstrel" ride the same channel — full-length terms the 100-character field cannot afford.
- No plurals of included singulars, no club/league proper nouns, and no tournament marks in consumer-visible copy (Guideline 2.3.7) — the description says "svetový turnaj", never the mark.
