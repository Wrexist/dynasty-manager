## Keyword rationale
- **WC-2026 refresh (2026-07):** added **`gestionale`** and **`simulatore`**, dropped **`formazione`** and **`campionato`**. This is the highest-value edit in the set:
  - **`gestionale`** is *the* Italian word for a management sim and the category leader ships a fully localized Italian listing ("Manager di Calcio") — but the big English-metadata sims (Soccer Manager, OSM) run untranslated English into Italy and cannot rank on it at all. A genuinely Italian field wins this term uncontested by half the competitive set.
  - **`simulatore`** (simulator) is the depth-intent qualifier the arcade/on-pitch giants don't earn and the thin clones don't localize — it separates our management sim from match-play games in the exact terms Italian sim searchers use ("simulatore di calcio", "gestionale calcio").
  - Dropped **`formazione`** ("rosa" + "tattica" already cover lineup/squad-building intent) and **`campionato`** ("nazionale"/WC copy carries the tournament intent that matters in this window); the freed budget buys the two category-defining terms above.
- The listing is now on the 2026-27 club-season window, so no tournament token appears in any indexed field. The Subtitle spends all 27 characters on evergreen head terms (`Tattica, Mercato e Carriera`); "nazionale" in the keyword field keeps international-management intent covered without a mark.
- "calciomercato" is one of the highest-volume football words in Italy (its own media season) — non-negotiable even though it shares the "calcio" stem with the App Name, because Apple tokenizes it as a distinct compound users actively search.
- Authentic terminology beats translation: "mister" (what players call the gaffer), "rosa" (squad), "rigori" (penalties/shootout), "nazionale" (national team), "esonero" (sacking, in the Description). This is the language of Italian football media, not a dictionary render.
- "allenatore" anchors the Subtitle as the formal manager term, complementing the colloquial "mister" in keywords; the no-subscription flank against the orphaned "football manager" brand lives in the Promo/Description ("senza abbonamento"), where it converts, rather than in the search field where "gestionale"/"simulatore" have far more query volume.
- Excluded real league names (e.g. the top-flight trophy name), club and player names, and competitor names for IP safety; excluded "calcio", "tattica", "mercato" and "carriera" from keywords as they already sit in Name/Subtitle.
- If a future tournament window reopens this angle, use the generic "torneo internazionale" or "nazionali" — never "Coppa del Mondo" or a capitalised "Mondiale", both of which are marks in listing copy (`APP_STORE_LISTING.md` risk #4).
