## Keyword rationale
- Italian fans call the World Cup "il Mondiale" in speech but type "coppa del mondo" as the search head term — so "coppa del mondo" leads the keyword field while "Mondiale" carries the Subtitle, covering both without duplication.
- "calciomercato" is one of the highest-volume football words in Italy (its own media season) — non-negotiable inclusion even though it shares the "calcio" stem with the App Name, because Apple tokenizes it as a distinct compound users actively search.
- Authentic terminology beats translation: "mister" (what players call the gaffer), "rosa" (squad), "formazione" (lineup), "rigori" (penalties/shootout), "campionato" (league title race), "nazionale" (national team), "esonero" (sacking, used in the Description). This is the language of Italian football media, not a dictionary render.
- "allenatore" anchors the Subtitle as the formal manager term, complementing the colloquial "mister" in keywords.
- Excluded real league names (e.g. the top-flight trophy name), club and player names for IP safety; excluded "calcio/allenatore/mondiale/2026" from keywords as they already sit in Name/Subtitle.
- Safe alternate WC phrasing for near-zero IP exposure: "Campionato del Mondo" or the standalone "Mondiale" in place of "Coppa del Mondo".
