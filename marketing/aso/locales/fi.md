## Keyword rationale
- The native head compound "jalkapallomanageri" keeps the **Subtitle** lead — it is the exact term Top Eleven uses as its Finnish title, and Finnish agglutination means Apple will not assemble it from the separate "Jalkapallo" and "Manager" tokens in the App Name. Finnish is the lowest-competition localized set in this cluster, so owning the term is cheap and high-return.
- The tournament stamp that used to share the subtitle is gone (that window closed 19 July 2026). Its characters now go to "koko kausi": season intent that reads as a benefit, which is what the Today-tab ad format needs — it renders only name + icon + subtitle.
- The English head term "manager" is carried by the English App Name, and "jalkapallomanageri", "Jalkapallo" and "kausi" now sit in Name/Subtitle — so the keyword field is all native Finnish.
- Two additions funded by the reclaimed characters: "manageripeli", how Finns name the genre in search and a string no Name/Subtitle recombination produces, and "liiga" — the English loan Finns type as often as the native "sarja", which is kept alongside it. "peli" stays because it recombines with the name's "Jalkapallo" to cover "jalkapallopeli".
- "rangaistuspotku" is dropped from the keyword field and moved into the screenshot captions. Penalty shootouts are a marquee *visual* on a narrow query, and the word cost 15 of 100 characters; captions have been an indexed field since June 2025, so it earns more there.
- "kokoonpano", "joukkue", "siirrot", "maajoukkue", "seura" are the exact Finnish words for lineup, team, transfers, national team and club — base (nominative) forms chosen so Apple's imperfect Finnish tokenizer matches them cleanly. "sarja", "ura", "taktiikka", "valmentaja" are the high-volume heads: Finns search the coach term as readily as the manager term.
- Description keeps the local-relevance hook but phrases it generically ("Suomen pääsarjasta") instead of naming any real league or club — the home-market pull Soccer Manager 26 and OSM get from real clubs, achieved here trademark-free. No real league or club appears in any store field.
- The old "FUT-tyyliset" phrasing in the pack bullet is gone: it borrows a competitor's mode abbreviation for no search benefit (the description is not indexed) and is a needless 2.3.7 exposure.
