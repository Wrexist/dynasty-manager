## Keyword rationale
- Name/Subtitle words are not repeated: 足球經理 (Name, covers 足球/經理) and 新賽季·開幕·戰術·轉會·青訓·生涯·執教·球會·奪冠 (Subtitle) are already indexed as one union, so keywords carry only distinct terms.
- 足球經理 in the Name is the exact genre label TW/HK players type for football-management games. Competitor evidence: Top Football Manager's TW title appends "- 足球經理" and the fantasy-manager 2025夢幻足球經理 leads on the same phrase, confirming 足球經理 as the TW/HK head keyword — owning it in the Name anchors category search. Unchanged by the season pivot.
- **Subtitle re-pointed at the season window (was the tournament line), and it now runs at 24/30 instead of 21/30.** Traditional Chinese is character-dense, so the extra room buys whole head terms rather than syllables. The incumbents (Top Eleven, Top Football Manager, 2025夢幻足球經理) stay evergreen and never lead on a *season*, so the line opens on 新賽季開幕 (season kickoff, the August intent), stacks four terms TW/HK players actually type (戰術, 轉會, 青訓, 生涯), and closes on the benefit 執教球會奪冠 for the Today-tab ad format, which renders only icon + name + subtitle. 球會 — not mainland 俱樂部 — keeps the head term in the regional idiom.
- Characters reclaimed from the tournament: the tournament token, the year and 國家隊 left the Subtitle, and 轉會·青訓·生涯·執教·戰術·球會 left the keyword field (now covered by the Subtitle). That bought 主帥 (the TW/HK head-coach noun alongside 教練/領隊), 國家隊 (back into keywords now that the Subtitle no longer carries it), 保級 (relegation battle, pairing with 升降級), 換人 (substitutions), 引援 (signings — worth restoring now that 轉會 has moved to the Subtitle), 免費, 先發 (starting XI), 新星 (wonderkid) and 合約 (contract, distinct from the negotiation verb 簽約).
- 十二碼 and pk大戰 were **dropped from the keyword field and moved into caption 2.** Captions are OCR-indexed since June 2025, so the two regional shootout idioms earn more as visible screenshot text than as ten characters of a 100-character field — and the field now spends those characters on season and management intent instead.
- 無體力限制 has left the Subtitle. The no-timer wedge is a *conversion* argument, not a query: nobody searches it, so it now leads the Promotional Text and gets its own paragraph in the description opener, where it flanks Top Eleven's silent energy/token grind at the install decision rather than burning indexed characters.
- Vocabulary stays deliberately Traditional/HK-TW: 陣容/陣型, 球探 (scout), 養成 (development), 升降級/保級, 教練/主帥/領隊, 球隊 (team, distinct from the Subtitle's 球會), 外圍賽 and 十二碼 in body copy.
- 模擬經營 and 戰略 target the management/strategy-sim browse cluster; 卡包 ties pack-opening to the card-collecting cluster; 冠軍 and 名門 add trophy/prestige intent; 洲際賽 rides continental-cup intent without naming a real competition.
- Loop terms: 租借 (loan-deal system), 簽約 (career negotiation), 比賽 (match — core sim word), 訓練, 經營遊戲 (management-game genre-browse phrase; 經營 is NOT in the Name, which carries 經理).
- 領隊 is retained — the TW manager/team-leader term Top Eleven uses in its own TW title ("…成為全球最佳足球領隊") — alongside 教練, the emotional verb 2025夢幻足球經理 leads with ("像教練管理"); both are generic descriptors, not brand strings.
- Cross-store note: zh-Hant only resolves on TW/HK/MO — the Simplified forms (俱樂部/点球/妖人) belong to zh-Hans and are omitted here so neither localization wastes characters on the other's script or wrong-region vocabulary.
- No tournament mark appears anywhere in consumer-visible copy (Guideline 2.3.7): the national-team lane is carried by the generic 國家隊 / 國際大賽 / 洲際賽.
