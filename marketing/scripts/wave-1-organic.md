# Wave 1 — Organic launch week (posting kit)

Six posts, zero spend, three platforms. This week does two jobs at once:
warms the brand-new accounts (early posts on a fresh account are throttled —
the clock starts at the first upload, so start now) and runs a free four-hook
test whose read decides what the first creator brief and the first paid dollar
point at.

**Platforms:** TikTok + YouTube Shorts + Instagram Reels — same file, all
three, same day. **Music:** added in-platform, never baked into the file; on
TikTok use only the Commercial Music Library so any post stays boostable as an
ad later. Sync the drop to the walkout flash (~7s on the pack cuts).
**Captions below are the in-platform text**, not burned into the video.

## The read (this is the whole point of the week)

After 48h per post, record per platform:

- **3-sec view rate** — the hook score. ≥25% healthy; a hook under 20% at
  ~5K views is dead, stop using it.
- **Average watch %** — the body score. Under ~50% on a 13s cut → the middle
  drags, cut tighter next round.
- **Comments** on the bait posts — the only metric where the *reply speed*
  is an input: first-hour replies are an algorithmic signal, not manners.

The winning hook becomes: the creator brief's angle (tiktok-02), the Meta
port (ad-meta), and — only once page CVR ≥ 25% — the $300 paid test
(tiktok-08's plan). One winner, three reuses.

## Guardrails (same as the whole kit)

- Real player names in FOOTAGE: fine — it is the product. In captions,
  hooks, or hashtags: never.
- No fail-ad bait, no fake gameplay, no "iOS only" apologies in copy.
- If a post unexpectedly runs (>50K views), do not delete or edit it — edits
  reset distribution. Post the follow-up as a reply video instead.
