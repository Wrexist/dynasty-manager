# TikTok-07 — Lo-Fi "Flow State" Selfie Meme (BitLife-style)

**Format:** Spark Ad (boost an organic creator post) — this format lives or
dies on looking like a random person's post.
**Length:** 6-10 seconds
**Aspect:** 9:16 (1080×1920)
**Effort:** UGC creator $200-500, or self-shot in 10 minutes. Deliberately bad.

## Pitch

Clone of the BitLife "When I'm playing BitLife and I become a famous football
player who constantly cheats on his wife and i genuinely reach flow state" ad
(~200K likes as a paid placement). The formula:

1. **A hyper-specific confession caption** naming the game *in the text*
   ("When I'm playing Dynasty Manager and..."), escalating through 2-3
   absurdly specific clauses, landing on a deadpan self-aware punchline
   ("...and I genuinely reach flow state" energy).
2. **Footage that is aggressively nothing**: a lo-fi selfie clip, bad
   lighting, lens flare, the person barely reacting or mouthing along to a
   sound. The gap between the epic text and the mundane face IS the joke.
3. The game never appears on screen. Only the App Store card sells.

This is the cheapest format in the kit — no gameplay capture at all.

## Creator-shoot brief (paste into DM)

> Film a 7-second selfie clip, handheld, whatever lighting you have — worse
> is better. Sit there like you're mid-thought, tiny smirk at the end, maybe
> a slow nod. That's it. Don't act. We'll add the text. Use whatever selfie-
> meme sound is trending for you this week, or we'll pick one. Sponsored tag
> mandatory. $X + Spark posting rights.

## Body

| Time | Beat |
|------|------|

No CTA inside the video. The ad card does the selling.

## Music / sound

- **Sound is the other half of this format.** Use a trending
  selfie-meme/"flow state" audio — the lo-fi chill beat or spoken-word meme
  sound currently circulating in gaming TikTok. Check TikTok Creative Center
  → Trending (last 7 days, target country) the day you post; this format's
  sounds rotate weekly, so the script deliberately doesn't hardcode one.
- If the creator posts organically first (Spark flow), **let them pick** —
  they know their audience's current sound better than any brief.
- Sound-off is still safe: the text carries the joke.

## Production realism check

- Would a real football fan share? ✅ (tag-a-mate mechanics: "this is you")

## Why this should convert

- The reference ad is a proven paid creative (~200K likes) in the identical
  niche, with production cost near zero.
- Naming the game inside the meme text is the trick: the "ad" is consumed as
  a relatable joke, and brand recall comes free.
- Five text variants on one 7-second clip = the cheapest hook-rate test in
  the entire kit; winners port directly to tiktok-06 POV texts and Meta.
