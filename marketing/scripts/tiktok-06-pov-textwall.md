# TikTok-06 — POV Text-Wall over Match Drama (BitLife-style)

**Format:** In-Feed first (this format needs curated footage, not a creator's
face) — then Spark-boost the organic upload from the game's own account.
**Length:** 9-13 seconds
**Aspect:** 9:16 (1080×1920)
**Effort:** Self-produced in ~1 hour (screen recording + text overlay). $0 creator cost.

## Pitch

Direct clone of the BitLife "POV: you prepared your entire life for the World
Cup just to lose to an 18 year old statmaxing prodigy" ad (~100K likes as a
paid placement). The formula:

1. **A wall-of-text POV caption** that collides *game-mechanic language*
   ("statmaxing", "regen", "week 38") with *real-world emotional stakes*
   (World Cup, getting sacked, your wonderkid leaving). The text IS the ad.
2. **One continuous, tension-frozen piece of footage** underneath — a keeper
   mid-dive, a penalty run-up, a last-minute goal. No cuts, no logo, no UI
   tour. The game only appears in the App Store card below the video.
3. Zero production value signals. It reads as a meme, not an ad.

Timeliness: the real 2026 World Cup is on air NOW (through mid-July). WC-themed
POV text rides the biggest football conversation of the year and matches the
v1.2.0 "World Cup update" ASO push.

## Footage (pick one, screen-record in-app)

The game is silent — all audio is added in the edit (see Music).

| Source | How to capture |
|--------|----------------|
| **Penalty shootout** (best — matches the BitLife frozen-keeper energy) | WC mode, play to a knockout shootout, screen-record the kick sequence. Slow to 0.5× in edit at the keeper's dive. |
| **2.5D pitch goal + slow-mo replay** (shipped v1.2.0) | Any WC match at Normal speed, capture the goal moment: stadium flash + instant replay. |
| **Trophy lift** (v1.2.0) | Win the WC final, capture the trophy-lift celebration screen. Use as the *last* 1-2s only. |
| AI bookend (BitLife's actual approach) | `marketing/ai-prompts.md` → Runway/Veo prompt for a broadcast-style keeper dive / stadium shot, then hard-cut to game footage for the final 2s. |

Crop the recording so no iOS chrome shows. Do NOT show menus. One moment,
held. The viewer should be unsure for 5 seconds whether it's a game at all.

## Music / sound

- **Default: a trending "dramatic/emotional" sound.** Pick from TikTok
  Creative Center → Trending Sounds (filter: your target country, last 7 days)
  the week you post — do not hardcode a track name into the edit; trends
  decay in days. The BitLife original ran a slow, cinematic build.
- **Sound-off safety:** the text wall delivers 100% of the joke with sound
  off. That's why this format works as a paid placement.
- **DIY alternative:** stadium-ambience + single commentator line ("...and
  it's the 18 year old!") from a stock SFX pack, ducked under the trending
  audio. The game itself has no audio, so nothing to capture in-app.

## CTA — soft, native

- Ad-card copy (TikTok Ads Manager): "Vill du prova det först? Spela nu"
  equivalent — the platform renders the App Store card; keep the video itself
  CTA-free except the final "search Dynasty Manager" text swap.
- Never "Download now" inside the video.

## Production realism check
