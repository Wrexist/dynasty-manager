# TikTok-02 — Pack-Opening Reaction (Spark Ad on real reaction)

**Format:** Spark Ad — boost an existing creator's pack-opening reaction
**Length:** 12-18 seconds
**Effort:** Find creators already posting FC/EA SPORTS FC pack-opening content, brief them to do the same in Dynasty Manager. Pay $200-500 per Spark-eligible post.

## Pitch

The single highest-converting TikTok format for sports games is the
genuine pack-opening reaction. EA FC creators have built entire careers on
it. Get one of them to open a Rare Gold or Icon pack in Dynasty Manager,
react authentically. The reaction IS the ad. The walkout animation does the
visual work. The creator's existing follower base does the social proof.

## Creator-shoot brief

> Hey [creator] — fan of your FC pack-opening content. We make Dynasty
> Manager, a free-to-play football manager game with a pack system. Free
> daily packs, paid Premium/Rare Gold/Icon tiers with walkouts (~$3 to $10).
>
> Brief: open a Rare Gold or Icon pack on stream/phone. React genuinely.
> Don't fake it. If the pull is mid, your honest reaction is still useful —
> we'd rather see disappointment than fake hype.
>
> Shoot phone-vertical, half-face / half-pack-animation. 12-18 seconds.
> Sponsored disclosure mandatory.
>
> Pay: $X for the post + Spark Ad rights for 90 days. We send you the
> Spark code; you just authorize it in your TikTok app.

## Hook (0-1s) — 3 variants the creator should naturally do

| ID | Visual | Creator's first words (caption follows) |
|----|--------|------------------------------------------|
| A  | Pack tier name "RARE GOLD" on screen + creator's face anticipating | "...let's do it." |
| B  | Pack just starting to shake (charge phase) | "no, no, no, no..." |
| C  | Black for 0.3s → walkout flash hits | "WAIT." |

## Body — let the creator drive it

- Don't script the middle. The pack-opening overlay drives it.
- Pre-arranged: the pack runs 1.5s loading → 0.5s portal → 0.6-1.5s charge → 0.7s explode → reveal stagger (~2s) → walkout (2.2s) → summary.
- Creator's reaction lands at the explode (00:04-00:05) and walkout (00:07-00:09) moments.

## CTA — soft

Creator's choice. Recommend they end with:
- "free daily packs btw"
- "this is free, mate"
- "search Dynasty Manager"

## Music

Creator's choice. EDM build-drop is the genre-standard, beat-drop synced to
the explode moment. They know this — they make this content for a living.

## Capture instructions — DON'T edit the raw

The whole value of Spark is the original creator's authentic post. Do not
re-cut, do not add brand chrome, do not change the caption. Just authorize
the Spark code and let it run as boosted organic.

## Why this should convert

- **Spark + UGC + reaction** = the three highest-impact levers for TikTok sports-game UA.
- **Pack-opening is a known content category** — viewers self-select, prequalified intent.
- **Creator's existing audience** front-loads the social-proof signals (likes, comments) the algorithm uses to decide reach.
