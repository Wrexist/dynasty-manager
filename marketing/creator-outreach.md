## Target Creator Tiers

| Tier | Subscribers | Strategy | Expected installs / video |
|------|-------------|----------|--------------------------|
| **Micro** | 1k–20k | Easiest to land, fastest response, very engaged audiences | 50–300 |
| **Mid** | 20k–200k | Best ROI — still reachable, meaningful reach | 300–3,000 |
| **Macro** | 200k–1M | Hard to land without budget, but one hit changes everything | 3,000–30,000+ |
| **Mega** | 1M+ | Needs payment or PR agency — skip for now | — |

**Start at Micro + Mid.** Land 10–15 videos, collect social proof, then use that as leverage for bigger creators.

## What to offer (in order of preference)

1. **Free Dynasty Pro key** — costs you nothing, high perceived value
2. **Early access** to the next build with unreleased features
3. **Co-create content** — "suggest a challenge and I'll build it into the game"
4. **Credit in the app** — "thanks to [creator]" in the settings/credits for the biggest ones

**Never offer money upfront to creators you're cold-DMing** — it triggers FTC disclosure requirements they may not want to handle, and it reads as desperation to the better creators. Build the relationship first.

## Content angles to suggest (if they ask for ideas)

- "I took [lowest-rated club in their country] to the Champions League"
- "Can you win the league without spending any money?"
- "I got sacked 5 times and came back to win it all"
- "Managing [their favourite real club] — is it realistic?"
- "Every club in [their country's league] — rated"
- "I gave myself 5 seasons to win with [difficult club]"

These work as YouTube series, TikTok series, or one-off Reels. Give creators the angle, not just the app.

---

## Disclosure

**Always ask creators to disclose** that you gave them free access — "gifted" or "#ad" depending on the territory. In the UK, ASA requires this. In the US, FTC requires it for material connections. Asking upfront protects both of you and builds trust.
