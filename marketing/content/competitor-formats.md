# Competitor short-form formats → Dynasty Manager ideas

Researched 2026-09-27. Feeds `30-day-calendar.md` (weeks 2 to 4) and PLAYBOOK §2 and §5.

**How to read the evidence.** TikTok pages block fetching, so every TikTok page
below was seen only as a **search-result title**. Titles show that a format exists
and roughly what it is. They do not show views, and none were watched.
Tags used:

- **[SRC]**: backed by an article, a store listing or a YouTube playlist that was read.
- **[TITLE]**: seen only as a TikTok or YouTube title. The format exists; no view counts.
- **[UNVERIFIED]**: my inference from genre knowledge. Check in TikTok search before relying on it (PLAYBOOK §2.3, 45 minutes).

Rules that apply to every idea below:

- A pinned or re-rolled capture (`hero=`, `minHero=`, `legend=1`) is a **showcase**.
  Never call it "my pull", "rate my pull" or luck (PLAYBOOK §4, 2026-09-27).
- Every post uses **Your brand** disclosure, so the only sounds allowed are the
  Commercial Music Library or the owned score.
- Burned-in text has no emoji. Use 3 to 5 hashtags, with no competitor or league marks.

## 2. Harness facts that shape the ideas (read from `origin/main`, 2026-09-27)

1. **Walkout beats** (from `PACK_ANIM.walkout` and the overlay). Times are page seconds on the capture clock, **estimated** for a 90+ Legends (icon) card:
   portal and arrival about 0 to 1.3 · charge 1.3 to 3.9 · auto-rip 3.9 to 4.4 · burst to about 4.8 ·
   stage and plinth about 4.8 to 5.5 · **nation 5.5 · position 6.2 · rating count 6.9 to 7.7** ·
   breath to about 8.0 · **flip and name about 8.0 to 8.9** · hold with potential bar about 8.9 to 11.2.
   ±1 s. Verify against `times.json` and a contact sheet before setting caption times.
2. **The icon plan stops at `wait(9000)`**, which is right at the estimated flip. The
   new flip landing is the last frame, and the hold and **potential bar are not captured**.
   The guess formats below end on the reveal on purpose, which loops well. Anything
   that needs the hold uses the **pack** plan, which waits 15 s. Extending the icon wait to about
   12,000 is a one-line change in `capture-ad.mjs`, left for the owner to decide.
3. **Burned-in captions (`hook` / `mid` / `cta`) render only in the pack scene.**
   `scene=squad` supports only the `pov` wall.
4. **`scene=transfer` and `view=grid` are flagged "unusable at 9:16"** in
   `week1-production.json`. Ideas that need them are marked BLOCKED.
5. **Headless cannot show the swipe itself or play sound.** A tap triggers `tapToRip`,
   which runs the same tear on its own, and the screencast has no audio. The finger-swipe and
   rip-ASMR formats are owner recordings.
6. **Star renders made before #633** (merged as 9aab6a47) show the old walkout, which spelled out the name
   before the flip. They cannot be used for guess formats. Re-render.
7. Candidate heroes checked in `nationalPlayerPool.ts` and `playerPortraits.ts`.
   None of them is in the calendar yet:
   Saka (88, ENG RW, portrait), Musiala (88, pot 92), Wirtz (89, age 23, pot 93),
   Rúben Dias (86, POR CB), Lewandowski (88), De Bruyne (87). **Yamal has no portrait, so do not use him.**

## 4. Top 6 headless: capture specs

Prefix every query with `http://127.0.0.1:8090/capture.html?`, then run
`node marketing/postproduction/capture-ad.mjs <dir> "<url>" 6 <plan>`.
**Caption times are estimates (§2.1).** Check the contact sheet, and move
`hookUntil` / `midFrom` / `ctaFrom` if a scrim covers the tear seam, the clue row
or the name.

### 1. Guess the Player #1 (plan `icon`)
- **Query**
  `tier=icon&hero=bukayo%20saka&hook=GUESS%20THE%20PLAYER%20BEFORE%20THE%20FLIP&hookUntil=3.8`
- **Hook** (0 to 3.8 s): GUESS THE PLAYER BEFORE THE FLIP. **Mid**: none; a caption would cover the clues. **CTA**: none. The take ends on the name, and the loop brings the hook back.
- **Caption** (no name; it would spoil the answer, so search gets the format term instead): `Guess the player from 3 clues: nation, position, rating. Showcase card in a free football manager game on iPhone. Answer in the comments #guesstheplayer #footballquiz #packopening #footballtiktok`
- Pin a comment with the answer after about 2 hours. Series: EP2 `hero=r%C3%BAben%20dias` (hard mode, a CB), EP3 `hero=robert%20lewandowski`.

### 2. Guess his rating (plan `icon`)
- **Query**
  `tier=icon&hero=jamal%20musiala&hook=MUSIALA%20WALKOUT%20-%20GUESS%20HIS%20RATING&hookUntil=3.8`
- **Hook**: MUSIALA WALKOUT - GUESS HIS RATING. **Mid / CTA**: none. The rating count at about 7 s is the payoff.
- **Caption**: `Musiala walkout in a free football manager game on iPhone. Showcase card, not a pull. Guess the rating before it stops counting #musiala #walkout #packopening #footballgame #footballtiktok`

### 3. Wonderkid walkout (plan `pack`, which is needed for the hold and potential bar)
- **Query**
  `tier=rare&hero=florian%20wirtz&hook=23%20YEARS%20OLD%20-%20WATCH%20THE%20POTENTIAL%20BAR&hookUntil=2.6&mid=WONDERKID%20CARD&midPos=top&midFrom=9.6&midUntil=12&cta=DYNASTY%20MANAGER%20%C2%B7%20FREE%20ON%20IOS&ctaFrom=13`
- **Hook**: 23 YEARS OLD - WATCH THE POTENTIAL BAR. **Mid**: WONDERKID CARD. **CTA**: DYNASTY MANAGER · FREE ON IOS.
- A 5-card World Class pack deals 4 cards before Wirtz. The re-roll makes him the closing card. Trim the reveals in the edit if they drag. Encode 0.3 to 13.6.
- **Caption**: `Wirtz walkout: 23 years old and his potential keeps climbing. Showcase card in a free football manager game on iPhone. Best wonderkid in the game? #wirtz #wonderkid #walkout #footballgame #footballtiktok`

### 4. The light tells you (plan `pack`)
- **Query**
  `tier=rare&minHero=90&hook=WATCH%20THE%20LIGHT%20WHEN%20IT%20TEARS&hookUntil=2.4&mid=THE%20COLOUR%20IS%20THE%20BEST%20CARD%20INSIDE&midPos=top&midFrom=3&midUntil=5.5&cta=DYNASTY%20MANAGER%20%C2%B7%20FREE%20ON%20IOS&ctaFrom=13`
- **Hook**: WATCH THE LIGHT WHEN IT TEARS. **Mid**: THE COLOUR IS THE BEST CARD INSIDE. **CTA**: DYNASTY MANAGER · FREE ON IOS.
- True by design: `PackRip`'s `innerLight` is the best pull's tier, "never a colour chosen to excite". The claim is safe. Frame it as how the game works, never as a tip that improves your luck.
- **Caption**: `The light that leaks out of the pack is the colour of the best card inside. Showcase open in a free football manager game on iPhone #packopening #walkout #footballgame #iphonegames`

### 5. "This player never existed" (plan `pack`)
- **Query**
  `tier=rare&legend=1&hook=THIS%20PLAYER%20NEVER%20EXISTED&hookUntil=3.4&mid=HALL%20OF%20LEGENDS%20CARD&midPos=mid&midFrom=9.6&midUntil=12&cta=DYNASTY%20MANAGER%20%C2%B7%20FREE%20ON%20IOS&ctaFrom=13`
- **Hook**: THIS PLAYER NEVER EXISTED. **Mid**: HALL OF LEGENDS CARD. **CTA**: DYNASTY MANAGER · FREE ON IOS.
- The harness clears `retiredLegends`, so the legend comes from the built-in set. Confirm on the contact sheet that the name is invented before posting; if it is a real player's name, the hook is false.
- **Caption**: `He never played a real match. Legends who retire in your save come back as Hall of Legends cards. Free football manager game on iPhone #halloflegends #packopening #managergame #footballtiktok`

### 6. Speed-build: best XI in 10 seconds (plan `squad`, 5 takes, cut in the edit)
Take the first about 2.3 s of each take (the plan taps a starter at 2.5 s).
- `scene=squad&view=pitch&fill=1&pov=Building%20the%20best%20XI%20in%20the%20game%7Cone%20card%20at%20a%20time&povPos=top`
- the same with `fill=4`, `fill=7`, `fill=9`
- final hold: `scene=squad&view=pitch&fill=11&pov=Rate%20this%20XI%201-10&povPos=top` (keep about 3 s)
- **POV text**: "Building the best XI in the game / one card at a time", then "Rate this XI 1-10". The squad scene has no hook/mid/cta.
- The XI is built from the top of the pool, not pulled. The caption says so.
- **Caption**: `The best XI you can build in this free football manager game on iPhone, one card at a time. Showcase squad. Rate it 1-10 and name the weak link #dreamteam #footballgame #managergame #footballtiktok`
