## Rules for every post

- Text hook on frame 1. No logo and no menu in the first 3 seconds.
- Captions on every line. 80% of views are muted.
- **No real player's name in the hook, the caption or the title.** Real faces
  may appear because they are in the game (PLAYBOOK §4, owner decision
  2026-09-25). Names stay out of our own copy.
- End on a question. Pin the comment: *"Free on iPhone — search Dynasty Manager 🏆"*.
- Post between 19:00 and 21:00 UK time. On a big match night, post 30 minutes
  after full time, not during the match.
- Hashtags (keep to 4–5): `#footballgame #packopening #footballtiktok #managergame #iphonegames`.
  Do not use competitor brand hashtags.

and installs that day in App Store Connect.

  format with the highest share rate.
- After **30 posts**: pick the top two formats. They are the Spark Ad
  candidates in PLAYBOOK §9, once the paid gates pass.
