Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026 - version 1.6.0

Dynasty Manager is a single-player football management game. Your career saves, squads, settings and progress are stored on your device. Purchases use Apple and RevenueCat as described below.

Game saves and choices

We do not upload your career saves to our servers. Saves remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them. Deleting the app or its local data can remove your saves. Device backups are controlled by your device and Apple settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat validates purchases, maintains subscription entitlements and helps us deliver and recover purchased content. Purchase records include product and transaction identifiers, purchase dates, entitlement status and a randomly generated app user identifier. RevenueCat also processes technical information needed to operate its service. We do not receive your payment card details.

We use purchase information to provide purchased features, resolve payment problems and understand purchase performance. This app does not provide RevenueCat with your manager name, career save, email address or advertising identifier. The app user identifier is pseudonymous; it is not a named game account.

Diagnostics, analytics and advertising

Sentry crash reporting is disabled in version 1.6.0. No error reports are sent to Sentry in this release. The in-game analytics event system has no production collection endpoint. Purchase reporting is available through Apple and RevenueCat. Rewarded advertising is disabled, and this version does not request an advertising tracking identifier or show third-party ads.

Service providers

Apple and RevenueCat process information under their own policies: Apple Privacy Policy and RevenueCat Privacy Policy. Opening an external website subjects that visit to the website provider's policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and your choices

Local game data remains until you delete it. Purchase and support records may be retained to provide entitlements, address support requests and meet applicable requirements. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings; deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles payment records and refund requests.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Contact us to request help with information we or our service providers process. We may need purchase identifiers to locate a record; please do not send payment card details or passwords.

Support and younger players

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and description of the problem. Younger players should ask a parent or guardian before making purchases or sending personal information. A parent or guardian can contact us about privacy concerns.

Contact and updates

Contact privacy@dynastymanager.app about privacy or support@dynastymanager.app for game and purchase support. We will update this policy when our practices change.
