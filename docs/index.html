07Principles

What we left out.

A premium career game shouldn't feel like a slot machine. Here's what you won't find in Dynasty Manager — by design.

01 — Absent Accounts No sign-ups. No email. Open the app, pick a club, start a career.
02 — Absent Servers Every save lives on your device. Works on the train, on a flight, anywhere.
03 — Absent Advertising No banner ads, no rewarded videos, no interstitials between matches.
04 — Absent Tracking No analytics SDKs. No third-party identifiers. We don't watch you play.
05 — Absent Microtransactions No card packs, no premium currency, no pay-to-win. One price. One game.
08Questions

The short answers.

A few you'll likely ask. Full answers, plus more, on the support page.

Is the game playable offline?

Yes — Dynasty Manager runs fully offline. There is no online mode, no leaderboards, no required network connection.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

Not in the current build. If that ever changes, we'll update the privacy policy and App Store disclosures first.

Where is my save game stored?

Inside the app's private sandbox storage on your device. It's never uploaded to a server.

Will there be an Android version?

We're focused on shipping a great iOS release first. Android is on the roadmap but doesn't yet have a release date.
