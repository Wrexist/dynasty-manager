01Features

A career simulation,
in your pocket.

Mobile-first. Premium dark interface. No accounts, no servers, no advertising — just deep football management you can pick up on the train and put down before the kettle boils.

01
⚽

Ninety-two clubs across four divisions.

From the Monarch Premier League down to the Foundation League — pick your starting point and rise (or fall) through the pyramid. Every club has its own colours, history, and reputation.

02
🎯

Live match engine.

Minute-by-minute simulation with key events, mid-match substitutions, post-match analysis, and the late drama you always remember — goals after the eighty-fifth.

03
📋

Deep tactics.

Seven formations, twelve positions, set-piece routines, mentality, pressing.

04
💼

Transfers & loans.

Two windows a season. Negotiate offers, structure loans, balance the wage bill.

05
🌱

Youth & development.

Scout regens, run a youth academy, and watch young players grow toward their potential while veterans gracefully decline.

06
🏆

Promotion, relegation & cups.

Auto-promotion, end-of-season playoffs, the FA-style domestic cup, and a Hall of Managers that remembers every legacy.
