Manage every angle

A complete career simulation, in your pocket.

Designed mobile-first with a premium dark interface. Pick up your career on the train, finish a transfer window on your sofa.

92 Clubs · 4 Divisions

Start in the Monarch Premier League or grind up from the Foundation League. Auto-promotion, end-of-season playoffs, and relegation battles every May.

Deep Tactics

Seven formations. Twelve positions. Mentality, pressing, width, set-piece routines, and per-player roles — set your side up the way you see the game.

Live Match Engine

Event-based, minute-by-minute simulation. Read the flow, throw on a sub, watch the game tilt. Late drama after the 85th still hits exactly the way it should.

Transfers & Loans

Two windows a season. Negotiate offers, structure loan deals, handle approaches for your stars, and keep the wage bill within the board's appetite.

Youth & Training

Run a youth academy, scout regens, and run weekly training. Young players grow toward potential; veterans gracefully decline. Personality and chemistry are real.

Cups & Silverware

The domestic cup runs the full season. Six rounds, two-legged semis, a one-off final. Win it from the second tier and your stock with the board doubles.
