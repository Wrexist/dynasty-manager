Dynasty Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 May 2026

Summary: Dynasty Manager is a single-player football management game. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. All your save data stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Dynasty Manager does not collect any personal information. The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any contact details to play.

2. Data Stored On Your Device

To let you continue your career between sessions, the app stores your game progress locally on your device using your operating system's built-in storage (for example, localStorage within the app's sandbox). This data includes:

  • Your chosen club, manager profile, and in-game settings.
  • Squad rosters, fixtures, results, and league standings.
  • Your tactical setup, training plans, and transfer history.

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage permanently deletes it.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or location.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • We do not show third-party advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties.

4. App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

The current build of Dynasty Manager does not request permission to track you. If a future version introduces optional advertising, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures before that change ships, and any tracking permission will require your explicit consent through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

5. Children's Privacy

Dynasty Manager is rated for general audiences and is suitable for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, we likewise do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Third-Party Services

Dynasty Manager runs entirely on your device and does not send data to third-party services. The app may rely on Apple or Google platform services (for example, App Store delivery, push-notification permission prompts, or in-app purchase processing if added in the future), which are governed by Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

7. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any personal information about you, there is no personal data on our servers to access, correct, export, or delete. You can erase all data the app holds at any time by deleting the app or clearing its storage from your device's settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy, the new version will be posted at this URL with an updated "Effective date." Material changes will also be reflected in the App Store privacy disclosures before the affected version ships.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Get in touch: privacy@dynastymanager.app
