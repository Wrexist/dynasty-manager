# FC27 integration audit — 2026-08-28

> Audit of the FC27 data work against the shipped game, after the pack was
> regenerated. Every finding below was verified against code or data at the
> path named; nothing here is inferred from a commit message.

## Verdict

The club squads are real FC27 and correct. Three things are **not** consistent
with that, and one of them means "FC27 is in the game" is only true on one of
the two paths a player can take.

---

## F1 — Pack pulls were FC26 while squads were FC27  · **FIXED (9fe490a)**

`pickRealPlayerForPack` (`src/utils/realPlayerPicker.ts:111`) reads
`getNationalPoolSync()`, which resolves `src/data/nationalPlayerPool.ts` — and
that file's own header says *"top 220 real players per nationality from FC26
data. Auto-generated by scripts/buildNationalPool.mjs from FC26_20250921.csv."*

So a club's squad shows a player at his FC27 rating, and a pack deals the same
player at his FC26 rating. Two ratings for one person inside one save.

**Fixed:** `buildNationalPool.mjs` now takes `--csv`/`--out`, and the pool is
rebuilt from the FC27 export (84 nations, 8,994 players). `packs:supply` passes
— 10,055 templates, best player 91, every storefront band backed — it checks the
storefront's rating bands can actually be filled from the pool, and the pack
guarantees are stated against it.

**Risk:** the pack odds and the BEST VALUE badge are derived from this pool's
rating distribution. Changing the pool changes those, and `packs:supply` plus
the pack tests are what will catch it.

## F2 — The FC26 squads are the fallback layer everywhere  · **corrected; not a gap**

`initGame.ts:268` chooses the squad source:

```
const cpTemplates = communityPackEnabled ? cpByClub?.[club.id] : undefined;
const squad = cpTemplates?.length ? … : generateSquad(…)
```

and `generateSquad` reads `ALL_SQUAD_TEMPLATES` from `src/data/squads/*.ts`,
which is FC26-built.

**An earlier draft of this audit called that a hole. It is not.** `playerGen.ts:405`
is explicit: *"Real-player TEMPLATES (stats, positions, ages) are always used so
opt-out saves still feel like a curated football world. The `useRealNames` flag
only controls whether the template's real name survives."* So the three cases are:

| Path | Squad source |
|---|---|
| CP on, FC27 covers the club | FC27 real players |
| CP on, FC27 does not cover it | FC26 templates, real names |
| CP off | FC26 templates, aliased names |

Serbia, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Iceland and Israel therefore keep their full FC26
squads under every path — a check that first appeared to show the opposite was
measuring map membership rather than the code path.

Regenerating `src/data/squads/*.ts` from FC27 remains a real improvement — it
would lift the fallback layer to the current season — but it is an upgrade, not
a correctness fix.

**Shape of the upgrade:** regenerate `src/data/squads/*.ts` from FC27 via
`buildClubTemplatesFromFC26.mjs` (which now shares its name splitter with the
FC27 pipeline). It needs the FC26-shaped input the FC27 export already
produces.

**Risk:** 543 clubs have squads there against FC27's 532, so a straight swap
loses 11 clubs' real players on the non-CP path. A merge that keeps FC26 where
FC27 has nothing is the safer shape.

## F3 — Nationality labels broke national-team selection  · **FIXED (a76067e)**

`international.ts:679,734,736` filters with `nats.has(p.nationality)`, an exact
match. EA writes "Holland", "Korea Republic", "United States"; the game's
nations are "Netherlands", "South Korea", "USA".

Two faults: importing EA's labels verbatim regressed Netherlands and Czechia,
which the FC26 pack had right; and Korea/USA/Ireland/Ivory Coast/DR Congo were
broken in FC26 as well. 1,863 players are now eligible for their country.

## F4 — The club-vote gate discarded 461 good votes  · **FIXED (a76067e)**

The same aliasing. The gate compared raw nationality labels, so 408 Dutch, 239
Turkish and 88 Czech players were treated as identity mismatches.

## F5 — `src/data/nations.ts` carries duplicate nations  · **open, pre-existing, now load-bearing**

`NATIONS` contains BOTH `Turkey` and `Türkiye`, and BOTH `Czech Republic` and
`Czechia` — four entries for two countries. Whichever spelling a player
carries, half the squad pool for that country sits under the other entry.

Not introduced here and not caused by FC27 — but it bit during this work.
Canonicalising EA's "Turkey"/"Czech Republic" onto the baseline's
"Türkiye"/"Czechia" moved those players to the *other* duplicate entry.
`buildNationalPool.mjs` records that the in-game labels are "Turkey" and
"Czech Republic", which is what EA already writes, so those two are
deliberately NOT aliased (fixed in 9fe490a). Until the duplicates are merged,
half of each country's pool sits under an entry nothing points at.

## F6 — `cpLeagueSquads` comment was false  · **FIXED (9fe490a)**

`initGame.ts:222` says the two datasets *"cover disjoint club ids in
practice"*. They no longer do: FC27's `byClub` covers Argentina, MLS, Saudi and
K League at 100%, all of which `cpLeagueSquads` also covers. The merge order
(`byClub` last) means FC27 wins, which is correct — but the comment explains
the code by a fact that has stopped being true.

## F7 — `npm run validate-cp` is broken  · **open, pre-existing**

It reads `src/data/communityPack/newLeagues.ts`, which does not exist in `HEAD`
and is not produced by any current script. The command therefore cannot have
run successfully in some time. It is not part of `preflight`, so nothing
caught it.

## F8 — 224 game clubs have no FC27 players  · **open, partly unfixable**

Six leagues have no club data on EA's public endpoint at all — Serbia,
Bulgaria, Slovakia, Iceland, Israel, Brazil. No amount of matching creates
squads that are not in the source. The rest are scattered gaps in Czechia,
Greece, Ukraine, Croatia, Hungary, Cyprus and Finland.

On the community-pack path these clubs fall back to generated players; the 7
CP-only leagues additionally fall back to FC26 via `cpLeagueSquads`.

## F9 — No Icons  · **open, product decision**

There is no retired-legend pool: `fc25_icons.csv` does not exist, nothing in
`src/` references the scraper's output, and EA's ratings API has no Icons
(`iterations` and `classLevels` filter dimensions are both empty). The Legends
Pack caption was corrected in `d816daa` to describe what it actually deals.
SoFIFA and CMTracker both answer Cloudflare bot challenges from this network.

## F10 — `medicalLevel` test was passing on luck  · **FIXED (9fe490a)**

Swapping the player pool made it fail at `expected 17 to be less than 17`. The
investigation found the assertion is sound but was badly under-powered, and why
is worth recording: **medical level reaches only one of the two injury paths.**
Non-foul injuries have their probability cut (`match.ts:1905`, 0.02 down to the
0.005 floor at max level); foul injuries fire on a flat `FOUL_INJURY_CHANCE`
and are unaffected in occurrence — a physio does not stop a bad tackle, it
shortens the recovery. Fouls dominate, so the whole-squad reduction is ~3%,
invisible over 60 seeds. Raised to 400 (measures 119 vs 116) with the reasoning
in the test. **The injury system itself was not changed** — this is a
documentation and sample-size fix, not a balance change.

## F11 — Sources for the missing clubs and Icons  · **investigated, closed**

With full network access, several previously blocked hosts now answer.
`api.easysbc.io` is a working public JSON API (no auth); `fctoolshub.com`
responds. SoFIFA, FUTBIN, FIFA Index, FIFACM and futwiz all still return 403
Cloudflare bot challenges.

None of them solves either problem:

- **The 224 clubs are not a sourcing problem.** Of the FC27 players still
  unplaced, **zero** belong to a club in the six empty leagues according to the
  FC26 baseline. Those players are simply not in EA's FC27 database — not
  clubless like the Eredivisie were, absent. No matching improvement and no
  second source of *EA* data can conjure them.
- **easysbc serves Ultimate Team card data**, not career ratings: records carry
  `resourceId`, `versionId`, `metaRating`, `priceInfo`, and ratings of 97-99
  for promo cards. There is no `potential`. Importing those into career squads
  would put materially wrong numbers in front of the player.
- **Icons were never blocked by access.** They are EA's licensed product — the
  retired-player likeness and name rights are the whole substance of an Icon.
  Shipping them as the headline of a paid pack is a rights decision for the
  owner, not a scraping problem, and it is a larger exposure than the caption
  that was already corrected. My recommendation is not to.

Bulk-harvesting a third party's compiled database into a shipped commercial
product is also outside what these sites' robots signals contemplate:
easysbc allows crawling for search, and fctoolshub explicitly signals
`ai-train=no, use=reference`.

### On item 5, the one that is left

`src/data/squads/*.ts` is the fallback layer, and it is FC26. Lifting it to
FC27 must be a **merge, not a replace**: it carries 543 clubs against FC27's
532, and five leagues (Serbia, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Iceland, Israel) exist there
and nowhere else. A straight regeneration would delete real squads for 16+16+
12+12+14 clubs to gain fresher data elsewhere — a bad trade.

It is deliberately not done in the same pass as everything above. It rewrites
46 generated files, and doing that at the tail of a long session is how a
regression gets shipped quietly.

## Not yet verified by anyone

- **No save has been loaded on a device** against the FC27 pack.
- **No season has been simulated** against these squads, so promotion,
  relegation, transfers and finances have not been exercised on real FC27
  ratings.

These remain the two checks that would most change confidence, and neither can
be done from here.
